# Incident response runbook

P1 incident response: The on-call engineer must acknowledge a P1 incident within 15 minutes. The incident commander posts a status update every 30 minutes until recovery. Escalate to the engineering manager if a P1 remains unresolved for 60 minutes.

P2 incident response: Acknowledge within 2 hours during business hours. The owning team provides a daily progress update.

Database recovery: Before restoring a database backup, record the incident ID and obtain approval from the database owner. Validate row counts and application health checks after restoration.
